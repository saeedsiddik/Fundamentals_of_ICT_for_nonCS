# Fundamentals_of_ICT_for_nonCS
# Fundamentals of Computer & Digital Literacy

**Course Code:** CSE-101 (or equivalent)  
**Instructor:** Md. Saeed Siddik  
**Department:** Institute of Information Technology (IIT), University of Dhaka  
**Audience:** Non-Computer Science Undergraduate Students  

Welcome to the course repository! This course is designed to transition you from being an expert *user* of technology into an *informed* practitioner. By exploring how internet systems work, how cybersecurity and IP laws function, how e-commerce operates, and how emerging technologies like AI impact society, you will gain the digital literacy needed for the modern professional world.

---

## 🎯 Course Overview & Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:
* **Understand Hardware & System Architecture:** Distinguish between PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN, client-server models, physical topologies, and core computing hardware.
* **Grasp Internet & Networking Fundamentals:** Explain how data moves across submarine cables, how DNS and IP addresses (IPv4/IPv6) function, and how caching/BDIX speeds up local content.
* **Navigate Internet Security & IP Rights:** Recognize social engineering (phishing, vishing, smishing), implement multi-factor authentication (MFA), and identify Intellectual Property frameworks like Copyrights, Patents, Trademarks, and GI tags in Bangladesh.
* **Analyze Online Business Models:** Evaluate how digital platforms monetize through Sales, Transaction Fees, Subscriptions, and Advertising models.
* **Assess Emerging Technologies:** Understand the foundations of AI, Machine Learning (ML), Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), and their societal/ethical impacts.

## 🛠 Handson Practical Exercises & Home Assignments

To solidify concepts covered in class, complete the following practical tasks:

1. **Network Diagnostics:**  
   * Find your device's physical Wi-Fi MAC address and local IPv4 address.
   * Query `"what is my IP"` on a web browser to check your public IP address. Explain why it differs from your private address.
   * Run a local speed test (BDIX) versus an international speed test and compare latency/throughput.
2. **Security & Phishing Audit:**  
   * Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on your institutional email and social accounts.
   * Analyze a suspicious email header and verify the destination URL using link checkers.
3. **Intellectual Property Case Study:**  
   * Select one official or candidate Geographical Indication (GI) product from Bangladesh (e.g., Haribhanga Mango, Katarivog rice, Tangail Porabarir Chomchom) and write a 1-page report on its commercial significance and IP protections.
